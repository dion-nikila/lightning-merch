Privacy policy

Collection preview · Updated 27 November 2026

This policy covers Bolt Space, the Lightning by Neesh merchandise preview. You can explore it and save pieces to a cart on your own device.

Your cart

Your browser stores the product number, size and quantity you choose. This lets the cart survive a refresh or a return visit in the same browser. The preview does not send those selections to the club. Remove items in the cart or clear this site’s browser data to erase them. Clearing site data may also reset other preferences for this site.

Hosting and tracking

GitHub Pages serves this preview. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. The storefront uses its own files for fonts and images and does not add advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics. Read GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contacting the club

If you message the club on Instagram, the crew can see your account name and the details you send and use them to reply. Instagram also processes that interaction under its own privacy policy. Please leave payment and identity documents out of messages. Contact the crew.

When ordering opens

The preview has no account, checkout or mailing-list forms. Before the shop asks for order or delivery details, it will show the privacy notice that applies to those details, including how they are used, shared and retained.
